APP NAME
FastTrack: Fasting Tracker

SUBTITLE
Build a sustainable rhythm

PLAY STORE SHORT DESCRIPTION
Track fasting, hydration, weight and progress in one calm, private app.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Build a fasting routine that fits your life with FastTrack.

Follow your fasting timer, understand general metabolic stages, record water and weight, and review your progress without pressure.

FEATURES

• Clear fasting timer
Choose a fasting plan and see elapsed and remaining time at a glance.

• Metabolic journey
Learn about anabolic, catabolic, fat-burning, ketosis and autophagy stages. Stage times are general estimates, not medical measurements.

• Flexible fasting plans
Use familiar schedules such as 12:12, 14:10, 16:8, 18:6, 20:4 and OMAD.

• Progress reports
Review completed fasts, consistency, duration and weight trends with readable charts.

• Water and weight tracking
Record hydration and body-weight entries without leaving the app.

• Helpful reminders
Receive optional fasting, phase, hydration and completion notifications.

• Your language and appearance
FastTrack follows your system language and light or dark appearance. English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, German and French are supported.

• Private by design
Core fasting, water, meal and weight records are stored on your device.

PREMIUM
Premium unlocks every fasting plan (18:6, 20:4, OMAD, 5:2, Eat-Stop-Eat, Warrior) and removes ads. Monthly, yearly and lifetime options may be available. Prices are shown by your store before purchase.

IMPORTANT
FastTrack provides general educational and wellness information. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Metabolic stage times are estimates and individual responses vary. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before fasting if you have a medical condition, take medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or feel unwell.
