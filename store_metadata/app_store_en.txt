App Name: FastLife - Intermittent Fasting
Subtitle: Fasting Tracker & Health Assistant

Short Description (Play Store - 80 chars):
Smart fasting tracker with metabolic phase analysis and health management.

Full Description:
Track your intermittent fasting journey like a pro with FastLife.

FEATURES:

• Smart Timer
Track your fast with a real-time circular timer. See your metabolic phases live - monitor fat burning, ketosis, and autophagy phases in real time.

• 8+ Fasting Plans
Choose from popular fasting plans including 12:12, 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, OMAD, 5:2, Eat-Stop-Eat, and Warrior diet, or create your own custom plan.

• Detailed Statistics
Analyze your weekly, monthly, and yearly fasting data with visual charts. Review metrics like streak tracking, completion rate, and average fasting duration.

• Metabolic Phase Tracking
Learn what stage your body's metabolism is at during your fast. Track anabolic, catabolic, fat burning, ketosis, and autophagy phases in real time.

• Meal Planner
Log what you eat during your eating window. Optimize your nutrition with calorie, protein, carb, and fat tracking.

• Weight Tracking
Monitor your weight changes on a chart. Measure your progress with BMI calculation and body metrics.

• Water Tracking
Easily log your daily water intake and reach your hydration goals.

• Smart Notifications
Stay on track with fasting start/end reminders, phase transition notifications, and motivational messages.

• Dark & Light Theme
Choose between eye-friendly dark theme and light theme options.

• HealthKit / Google Fit Integration
Automatically sync your steps, calories, and weight data with Apple Health or Google Fit.

• Turkish & English
Full Turkish and English language support.
