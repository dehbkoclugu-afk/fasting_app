# FastTrack competitive research — 2026-10-20

## Scope and sources

This review covers the current public product positioning of Fastic and Zero,
plus active Fastic creative patterns visible in Meta Ads Library on 2026-10-20.
Zero's name is too generic for reliable ad-level attribution in keyword search,
so no individual Meta ad is attributed to Zero here.

- Fastic product site: <https://fastic.com/en>
- Zero product site: <https://zerolongevity.com/>
- Meta Ads Library: <https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/>

## Product-positioning patterns

### Fastic

- Leads with a low-friction fasting journey rather than technical detail.
- Combines a fasting timer with food, water, steps, coaching, and an AI food
  scanner.
- Uses short benefit stacks and a direct start/download call to action.
- Reuses the same creative structure across localized language variants.

### Zero

- Leads with a simple fasting timer and sustainable routine building.
- Expands the story with meal tracking, protein, hydration, and habit support.
- Positions the product as an everyday health companion rather than only a
  countdown clock.

## Meta Ads Library observations

Active Fastic variants repeatedly used this structure:

1. A direct weight-management or transformation hook.
2. Three compact benefits, often AI food scanning, personalized support, and
   motivation.
3. Product/app footage in a short vertical video.
4. A single start-now or download call to action.
5. The same base concept localized into several languages.

Some visible creatives included guaranteed-result or short-term weight-loss
claims. FastTrack should not copy those claims. They create trust, policy, and
health-safety risk and are unnecessary for a strong launch message.

## FastTrack launch principles

- Use one hook, three concrete benefits, and one call to action per video.
- Show the timer or app interface in the opening seconds.
- Prefer 20–45 second vertical videos with readable on-screen captions.
- Reuse the visual structure across Turkish, English, and Brazilian Portuguese,
  while rewriting each caption naturally rather than translating word for word.
- Build recurring themes around routine, hydration, protein awareness, planning,
  and consistency.
- Use low-friction calls to action such as “Start with 12:12” or “Track today.”
- Describe tools and habits, not promised medical or weight-loss outcomes.
- Add “not medical advice” where content could otherwise be interpreted as
  individualized health guidance.

## Suggested first creative

**Hook:** Your fasting routine should be easy to see.

**Sequence:** Show the FastTrack timer, then three cards: fasting window,
hydration reminder, and daily consistency. End with the matching localized
profile handle and “Start with 12:12.”

This concept can be produced once and localized without making outcome claims.
